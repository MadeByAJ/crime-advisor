How your key is handled

The keyA custom key with log ticked (and Torn's public item list, for today's prices): each tool links to Torn's own form with that filled in. It can't see your money, stats, inventory or messages, and you can delete it in Torn any time.
Where it goesOnly to api.torn.com. The web apps' security policy blocks requests to any other address.
Where it's keptIn your browser: the tab only, unless you choose "remember". "Forget my key and data" removes it and every log a tool has read.
Your dataRead and worked out in your browser, and kept in its storage so it isn't downloaded twice. Never sent to us or anyone else.
The codeEverything runs in your browser, and all of it is public: github.com/MadeByAJ/crime-advisor.
